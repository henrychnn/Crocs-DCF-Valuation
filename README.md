
# Crocs Inc. (CROX) — DCF Valuation

## Project Overview

This project presents a five-year discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation of Crocs Inc., using FY2025 financial statements as the historical base and projecting financial performance through 2030.

The objective was to estimate Crocs' intrinsic equity value by analyzing historical financial performance, forecasting future free cash flows, and evaluating the company's cost of capital.

The model includes separate revenue forecasts for the Crocs and HEYDUDE brands, operating margin assumptions, capital expenditures, working capital projections, and a sensitivity analysis.

## Valuation Methodology

The valuation uses an unlevered free cash flow to the firm (FCFF) approach.

### 1. Revenue Forecasting
- Developed separate revenue forecasts for the Crocs and HEYDUDE brands.
- Incorporated historical revenue trends and 2026 management guidance.
- Projected consolidated revenue growth from 1.53% in 2026 to 2.84% in 2030.

### 2. Operating Income Forecast
- Analyzed historical operating margins and the impact of a $738.1 million impairment charge in 2025.
- Calculated impairment-adjusted 2025 EBIT to establish a normalized profitability reference.
- Forecasted EBIT margins ranging from 22.00% to 22.50% through 2030.

### 3. Free Cash Flow Projections
- Calculated NOPAT using projected EBIT and a normalized 23% tax rate.
- Forecasted depreciation and amortization, capital expenditures, and operating net working capital.
- Estimated annual FCFF from 2026 through 2030.

### 4. Weighted Average Cost of Capital
- Estimated the cost of equity using the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM).
- Estimated the cost of debt using historical interest expense and average interest-bearing borrowings.
- Calculated capital structure weights using market capitalization and interest-bearing debt.
- Derived a WACC of 10.89%.

### 5. Terminal Value and Equity Valuation
- Estimated terminal value using the Gordon Growth Model with a 2.50% perpetual growth rate.
- Discounted projected FCFF and terminal value to present value.
- Adjusted enterprise value for interest-bearing debt and cash to estimate equity value.
- Divided equity value by shares outstanding to calculate the implied share price.

### 6. Sensitivity Analysis
- Built a two-variable sensitivity analysis examining changes in WACC and perpetual growth assumptions.
- Evaluated implied share prices across WACC scenarios of 9.89%–11.89% and perpetual growth rates of 1.50%–3.50%.
- Calculated an implied terminal EV/EBITDA multiple as a valuation reasonableness check.

## Key Findings

The DCF model estimates Crocs' intrinsic value at **$144.91 per share** under the selected operating and valuation assumptions.

The forecast reflects modest revenue growth, operating margins stabilizing at 22.5%, and gradually increasing capital expenditures through 2030.

The valuation is particularly sensitive to long-term operating profitability and terminal value assumptions. Approximately 67.5% of enterprise value is attributable to the present value of terminal cash flows.

## Model Limitations

- The model uses FY2025 financial statements as its historical base while incorporating management guidance released in 2026.
- The 69.44% implied upside is measured against a December 2025 historical share price and should not be interpreted as a current investment return.
- Forecasted operating margins, revenue growth, capital expenditures, and working capital requirements are estimates subject to uncertainty.
- The cost of debt is estimated using historical borrowing expenses rather than a directly observed forward-looking market borrowing rate.
- Sensitivity analysis examines WACC and perpetual growth assumptions but does not capture every potential operating scenario.
